# GCD and Bézout certificate

Determine `gcd(84, 30)` and prove the result twice:

1. by a complete Euclidean-algorithm remainder chain; and
2. by back-substitution producing explicit integers `u, v` with `84u + 30v = gcd(84,30)`.

Verify that the claimed gcd divides both inputs and that every common divisor divides the claimed value.
