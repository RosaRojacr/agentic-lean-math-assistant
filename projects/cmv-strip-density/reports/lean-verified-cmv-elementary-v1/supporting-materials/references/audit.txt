MATHEMATICAL AUDIT OF CMV_Strip_Density_Problem.pdf

Verdict before Lean formalization
The intended elementary comparison argument is mathematically valid on this audit. The literal PDF theorem is NOT valid as printed: pages 2 and 3 contain an area-versus-perimeter typo. This audit is an analytic review with numerical diagnostics, not a claim of completed Lean verification.

1. Required theorem correction
Replace A_lambda(C_h)-P_lambda(E_s) by P_lambda(C_h)-P_lambda(E_s) in both Theorem 1 statements, including equation (3.2). The page-8 proof concludes the latter, correct quantity. This discrepancy was confirmed by viewing the rendered PDF, not merely its extracted text.
The former statement is genuinely false. For example, lambda=1.01 and h=1 give weighted area V approximately 3.706822640545596. Since 1<=f<=lambda, every finite-perimeter competitor of weighted area V satisfies
 P_lambda(E)>=P_Euclidean(E)>=2*sqrt(pi*|E|)>=2*sqrt(pi*V/lambda),
which here is approximately 6.791178545905693, greater than V. Thus the printed area-minus-perimeter expression is negative for every such competitor. The obstruction also follows without treating decimal computations as proof: at this rational lambda, A4(1)<(lambda+1)*pi+2<4*pi/lambda, using pi>3.

2. Analytic proof check
The identity B'+D=zD' gives S'=-2D. Evenness of D and the reflection identity for inverse trigonometric functions give S(-z)=lambda*pi-S(z). The endpoint values of J are both gamma. Gamma is positive because with theta=acos(1/lambda), gamma=lambda*theta-sin(theta)>0.
The displayed J'=4(D(2u-1)-D(u)) has the claimed sign change at u=1/3, because D is even and strictly increasing in |z|. This proves J>=gamma. For u>=1/3, J'<=0 implies J-uJ'>=J. For 0<u<1/3, D'(2u-1)<0 and D'(u)>0 imply J''<0, hence (J-uJ')'=-uJ''>0; the limit at zero is gamma. This proves both bounds over the full required domain. No square-root derivative at u=1 is used.
Direct substitution verifies both identities in Proposition 1, including their factors of h and h^2. Differentiating the displayed numerators verifies P3'=s*A3'. The positive numerator limit gives A3(s)->infinity at zero.
The largest-root construction is valid: the level set in [epsilon,h] is nonempty and compact. Its greatest root r lies strictly between the endpoints. Continuity and A3(h)<V imply A3(t)<V on (r,h], without requiring root uniqueness or global monotonicity. Therefore Q'=V-A3>0 on (r,h), and continuity at h gives Q(r)<Q(h), including h=1. Combining with the support gap proves the strict quantitative perimeter margin.

3. Geometry check
The attaching chords of the explicit caps and cores have exactly matching widths. Their interiors are interior points of the union, so no internal-chord perimeter is counted. The bottom segment of the three-arc competitor has density one. Both displayed weighted area/perimeter formulas have the correct factors.
For s<1/2, disk intersection with y>=1 really is the major segment. At s=1/2 all three arc centers coincide, giving the radius-two disk centered at (0,1), not a pinched region. The four-arc endpoint h=1 has a positive attaching chord and valid finite perimeter. The scalar endpoint A3(1),P3(1) is used only by continuity; the actual competitor always has r<h<=1.

4. Numerical diagnostics actually performed
70-digit arithmetic, 42 (lambda,h) pairs, including lambda=1+10^(-12), lambda=100, h=10^(-8), h near one, and h=1. The maximal residual in the two algebraic identities was approximately 4.64e-69. Equal-area roots gave the claimed strict gamma/h improvement in every sampled case. These samples do not prove universal assertions.
21 independent horizontal-coordinate area integrals checked the major-cap, semicircle, minor-cap and four-arc-endpoint cases. The largest relative residual against the stated scalar area formulas was approximately 1.47e-71.
At lambda=1.01,h=1, a sampled equal-area root is r approximately 0.9296553527250413. P4 approximately 6.852162236387912; P3(r) approximately 6.836009490135275; gap approximately 0.01615274625263771; gamma/h approximately 0.001873471126261384.

5. Original CMV conjecture versus Lean scope
The original CMV source supplies the necessary informal classification chain: Lemma 3.8 and its four-arc symmetry proof, Proposition 3.9 (every minimizer vertically reflection-symmetric), and Theorem 3.16. Thus the corrected explicit-family exclusion completes Conjecture 3.12 when these established source results are invoked.
For the explicit finite piecewise-circular Jordan regions, complete and reduced boundaries agree H^1-almost everywhere. To transfer from an arbitrary source minimizer, use its classified regular representative and invariance of REDUCED perimeter under area-null modifications. Do not assert that complete-frontier perimeter is invariant under arbitrary null modifications.
The draft should add this explicit corollary and reconcile page 2 with Remark 3. There is no need to claim the original source lacks classification. Preserve stadium / three-arc coexistence at the transition, not uniqueness.
The requested Lean run must distinguish its new explicit-family proof from an additional formalization of external BV/classification results. The latter are not silently assumed as axioms.
